Single Phase $I$ to $P$: $P = V \times I \times \cos\phi$

Three Phase $I$ to $P$: $P = \sqrt{3} \times V \times I \times \cos\phi$

$P_{\text{load}} = \sum (Power_i \times Qty_i)$

$P_{\text{inverter}} = P_{\text{load}} \times (1 + \frac{\text{Safety\_Margin}}{100})$

$\text{Energy (Wh)} = P_{\text{load}} \times \text{Backup\_Hours}$

$\text{Battery (Ah)} = \frac{\text{Energy}}{V_{\text{sys}} \times \frac{\text{DoD}}{100} \times \frac{\text{Efficiency}}{100}} \times \text{Aging\_Factor}$
